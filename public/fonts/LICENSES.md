Cobraa 3D font licences (static, subset)

The four faces used for the WebGL extruded preview are subsets of fonts from Google Fonts:

- Bricolage Grotesque (Bold) — OFL-1.1. See OFL-bricolagegrotesque.txt in /tmp/fonts3d or https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/bricolagegrotesque/OFL.txt
- DM Sans (SemiBold) — OFL-1.1. https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/dmsans/OFL.txt
- DM Serif Display (Regular) — OFL-1.1. https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/dmserifdisplay/OFL.txt
- DM Mono (Medium) — OFL-1.1. https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/dmmono/OFL.txt

The site also uses instrument-serif-regular.ttf (see OFL-instrument-serif.txt) and fonts served by Next/Google Font APIs. All of the above are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
