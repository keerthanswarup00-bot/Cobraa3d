Cobraa 3D font licenses (static, subset)

The four faces used for the WebGL extruded preview are subsets of fonts from Google Fonts. The corresponding SIL Open Font License 1.1 texts and copyright notices are included alongside the font files:

- Bricolage Grotesque (Bold) — `OFL-bricolagegrotesque.txt`
- DM Sans (SemiBold) — `OFL-dmsans.txt`
- DM Serif Display (Regular) — `OFL-dmserifdisplay.txt`
- DM Mono (Medium) — `OFL-dmmono.txt`

The site also uses Instrument Serif (subset at `src/fonts/instrument-serif.woff2`, source `instrument-serif-regular.ttf`; see `OFL-instrument-serif.txt`), Geist Sans/Mono (subset at `src/fonts/`, from the `geist` package) and fonts served by Next/Google Font APIs. All of the above are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
